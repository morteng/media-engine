## Brand Context

**Product**: Media Engine - An agent-based media production framework for automated content generation

**Brand Personality**: Professional yet approachable, technical but friendly, automation-focused, developer-centric

**Brand Colors** (use these exactly):

## Mascot Concept: "Capy" the Media Engine Capybara

### Why Capybara?
- Capybaras are known for being calm, friendly, and getting along with everyone (like our tool works with all content types)
- They're surprisingly capable and efficient (automated media production)
- They represent a chill, stress-free approach to complex tasks
- Unique and memorable - stands out from typical tech mascots

### Character Personality
- **Friendly & Approachable**: Warm, welcoming expression
- **Tech-Savvy**: Comfortable with technology, not intimidated by it
- **Helpful**: Always ready to assist with media production tasks
- **Calm & Capable**: Handles complex workflows effortlessly
- **Slightly Quirky**: Has personality, not generic

---

## Design Requirements

### Style Guidelines
- **Art Style**: Modern flat vector / geometric minimalist
- **Complexity**: Simple enough to work as small icon (16px) and large hero (512px+)
- **Lines**: Clean, consistent stroke weights
- **Shapes**: Geometric but soft, rounded corners
- **Expression**: Friendly eyes, slight smile, approachable

### Technical Specifications
- **Format**: SVG (vector) - REQUIRED
- **Color Mode**: RGB for digital use
- **Scalability**: Must work from 16x16 to 1024x1024+
- **Background**: Transparent

## Post-Generation Checklist

After generating assets with AI:
1. Clean up vectors in Figma/Illustrator
2. Ensure consistent stroke weights
3. Optimize SVG paths
4. Test at all target sizes
5. Create dark mode variants
6. Export in all required formats
7. Update brand.yaml with new asset paths

## Notes for AI Generation

- Run multiple iterations and select best results
- May need to combine elements from different generations
- Vector cleanup will likely be needed
- Consider hiring designer for final polish if AI results need refinement
- Test readability at small sizes before finalizing
